## Chosen Design: Sacred Manuscript / Dark Illuminated Codex

Deep black background, antique gold typography, Cinzel + EB Garamond fonts, asymmetric manuscript layout, grain texture, slow typewriter answer reveal. This feels most authentic to the subject matter — ancient, reverent, and mysterious without being theatrical.
