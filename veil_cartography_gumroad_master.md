## STEP 4 — FAQ (PASTE INTO GUMROAD FAQ SECTION)

**Q1. What exactly do I get when I buy a module?**
Each module includes a PDF workbook (concepts, explanations, journalling prompts, simple practices) plus an audio track to support guided reflection or a short contemplative practice. You can download the files and revisit them as often as you like.

**Q2. Who are these courses for?**
These mini-courses are for curious beginners, spiritual seekers, and people in the process of deconstructing or re-examining inherited religion. You do not need to identify as "Gnostic" to benefit — an interest in symbolism, inner work, or the Gnostic Gospels is enough.

**Q3. Do I need any prior experience or theological background?**
No. The materials are written in clear, accessible language with explanations of key terms. Where traditional or technical language appears, it is unpacked so you can follow along even if this is your first contact with Gnostic ideas.

**Q4. How long does each module take to complete?**
Modules are self-paced and designed to be completed in approximately 60–90 minutes, including reading and trying the practices. You can also spread the material over several days, revisiting the practices as often as you like.

**Q5. What format are the materials in?**
Each module is delivered as a downloadable PDF workbook plus an accompanying audio track (for a guided meditation, reflection, or practice). You can use any standard PDF reader and audio player on desktop or mobile.

**Q6. How long do I have access?**
Once you purchase, the files are yours to download and keep for personal use. You can re-download from your Gumroad library at any time using the email address you purchased with.

**Q7. Do I have to believe in a specific religion to use this?**
No. The approach is invitational, not doctrinal. You are encouraged to test ideas in your own experience and to keep what resonates, whether you are post-Christian, multi-tradition, "spiritual but not religious", or simply curious.

**Q8. Is this therapy, counselling, or a guarantee of results?**
No. These materials are educational and reflective, not a substitute for therapy, medical care, or professional advice. They are offered as maps and practices you can explore at your own pace; any outcomes will depend on your context, discernment, and ongoing inner work.
